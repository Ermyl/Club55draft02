Why Club 55 exists

Because retirement living in New Zealand has lost its balance — too expensive, too restrictive, and too late in life.

Club 55 was founded to offer something better: freehold homes at a fair price, transparent weekly costs, and a community designed for active people in their 50s and 60s. You keep control, capital, and choice — for a life that stays active, social, and your own.

💛 A life that feels good

Spaces to connect, move, and enjoy.

🛡️ Predictable weekly costs

Shared services for maintenance and common costs to keep life affordable.

🌿 Energy-smart by design

Insulation, solar options, and simple systems to lower power bills.

🗝️ Freehold ownership

You own your home outright — secure, independent, and yours to keep or pass on.

Location

Positioned between the coast and countryside, Te Horo offers a rare balance of peace and connection. Close to Wellington yet far from the rush, it’s a place for people who value space, nature, and small-town warmth — all within easy reach of family and everyday essentials.

Master plan

A human-scale village where everything’s a short, easy walk. Homes sit on quiet lanes with no-through traffic, a central green anchors daily life, and paths connect gardens, the community hub, and shared services. It’s designed for independence—low-maintenance, single-level living with plenty of places to meet, move, and breathe.

FAQ

Do I need to pay anything now?

No. We’re currently collecting expressions of interest to demonstrate demand and shape the first stage.

What will weekly costs include?

We intend to bundle common expenses (maintenance, shared areas, and rates/insurance where appropriate) to keep costs predictable.

Where is the site?

The land is under contract in New Zealand. Full location details will be shared with registered supporters first as plans firm up.

What’s the timeline?

The next step is preparing a Fast Track Approval (FTA) submission. Your registrations help us move faster.

Who we are

Club 55 is a new kind of community for New Zealanders over 55 — created by local developers who believe independence shouldn’t come with isolation or inflated costs. We build smaller, smarter homes with fair shared services so you can live well and spend less.
